🔒 Privacy Policy & GDPR Compliance

Ethiopia Data Protection Proclamation 1321/2024

1. Data We Collect

  • Business transaction data (revenue, cost, inventory)
  • Customer information (PII auto-masked)
  • IoT sensor data (temperature, humidity, pressure)
  • Usage analytics (non-personal)

2. GDPR Rights

  • Right to Access: Data keessan ilaaluu
  • Right to Rectification: Data sirreessuu
  • Right to Erasure: Data haquu (forget me)
  • Right to Portability: CSV/JSON download gochuu
  • Right to Object: Processing irratti diduu

3. Data Security

  • AES-256 encryption at rest
  • TLS 1.3 encryption in transit
  • PII masking (auto-detect emails, phones, IDs)
  • Audit trail (every access logged)
  • Role-based access control (RBAC)

4. Data Retention

Data keessan yeroo barbaaddan delete gochuu dandeessu. Default: 7 years (financial), 2 years (analytics).
