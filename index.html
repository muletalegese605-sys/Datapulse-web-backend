Frequently asked questions

Is my data secure and isolated?

Yes. Each organization has completely isolated storage. Your data is stored under a unique key per org ID and is never shared.

Can I pay in Ethiopian Birr?

Absolutely. We support TeleBirr and EBC Bank transfers for ETB payments, plus PayPal for USD/EUR.

Does the AI work offline?

Yes! Our local AI answers questions about your data in 12 languages without any internet connection. You can optionally connect a custom AI endpoint for advanced responses.

What happens after the 14-day trial?

You'll be prompted to choose a plan. Your data is never deleted — you can activate any plan and pick up where you left off.
